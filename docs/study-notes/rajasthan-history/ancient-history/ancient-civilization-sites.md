- **Burial 13 urn inconsistency within ASI Part II:** Chapter 23's introductory summary (S6, printed p. 1430) says Burial 13 did not contain an urn, but its detailed entry (printed pp. 1444–1445) says a broken urn was found. - **Rice remains at कालीबंगा:** ASI Jodhpur Circle's museum page mentions rice among crops, but the ASI *Indian Archaeology 1974–75 — A Review* (printed p. 79) records Vishnu-Mittre and R. Keep the site-specific crop claim disputed; do not state rice as a confirmed कालीबंगा crop without resolving the primary archaeobotanical evidence.

- **Rice / horse gram scope:** the ASI Jodhpur Circle museum webpage lists rice and horse gram among crops at कालीबंगा, but the ASI Part II Chapter 14 passage discusses wheat, barley, pea and Cicer arietinum; its nearby footnote refers to rice and horse gram evidence from Kunal.
